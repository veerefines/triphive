## LinkedIn

Every group trip starts the same way: a chat thread, three competing spreadsheets, and nobody owning the plan.

TripHive is a shared trip planner for groups:

- One day-by-day plan, live for everyone. Join with a 6-character code.
- Each traveler sets their own pace, daily budget, interests and wake-up time.
- Hive checks the plan. It flags a 116-minute transit leg with 30 minutes free, a missing lunch stop, or a trek that starts before someone is up.
- Ask @Hive in the group chat for a café near your stop. Nothing changes until the group votes.
- Split the cost, check the weather, send the PDF.
- Travelling solo? Find people heading the same way and ask to join.

## Product Hunt

**Tagline:** Plan the trip together. Let Hive keep it realistic.

**Description:** TripHive turns a messy group chat into one shared trip plan. Everyone sets their own pace and budget. Hive, the built-in planning companion, checks travel times, meals and wake-up times, and posts real place cards in the chat. The group votes before anything changes. Solo travelers can find trips going the same way and ask to join.
